Welcome to EarthOnline Alarm Clock!

We (together with our affiliates, "we", "our" or "us") respect your privacy and are strongly committed to keeping secure any information we obtain from you or about you. This Privacy Policy describes our practices with respect to Personal Data that we collect when you use our mobile application and services (collectively, "Services").

1. Data We Collect

We collect personal data relating to you ("Personal Data") to provide a personalized wake-up experience:

Personal Data You Provide:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you set up your profile, we collect your codename and birthday. This allows the Service to personalize your alarm greetings and provide birthday-specific content.
  • User Content: We collect settings and preferences you input, such as alarm times, voice selection, and specific phrase preferences for your personalized wake-up calls.
  • Communication Information: If you contact us for support, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Other Information You Provide: We collect other information that you may provide to us, such as when you participate in our events or surveys or provide us with information to establish your identity or age (collectively, "Other Information You Provide").

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Location Information: With your permission, we collect approximate location data (GPS and network-based). We use this data solely to provide real-time local weather updates and environmental information as part of your morning alarm broadcast.
  • Technical Information: We collect Log Data including your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, and the time/date of your Service usage to ensure app stability and performance.
  • Cookies & Tracking: This Service does not directly use "cookies." We may use third-party analytics tools (like Firebase) that use cookies or device identifiers to help us understand how you interact with the app. You can choose to accept or refuse these cookies and will be notified when a cookie is sent to your device. Please note that refusing cookies may limit your ability to access certain portions of the Service.
  • Links to Other Sites: This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Please note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we recommend that you review the Privacy Policy of any such websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the Personal Data collected for the following specific purposes:

  • Personalized Experience: To generate customized voice broadcasts that greet you by your Codename, wish you a happy Birthday, and provide the Weather Forecast based on your current location.
  • Service Maintenance: To provide, analyze, and improve the functionality of EarthOnline Alarm Clock.
  • Product Development: To conduct research and develop new features, such as smarter alarm triggers or improved voice synthesis.
  • Safety & Security: To prevent fraud, protect the integrity of our systems, and comply with legal obligations.

We may also aggregate or de-identify Personal Data so that it no longer identifies you and uses this information for the purposes described above, such as to analyze the way our Services are being used, to improve and add features to them, and to conduct research. We will maintain and use de-identified information in de-identified form and not attempt to reidentify the information, unless required by law.

3. Data Disclosure

We do not sell your Personal Data. We may disclose your information in these limited cases:

  • Weather Service Providers: We share necessary location parameters with third-party weather data providers to retrieve the forecast for your city.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with vendors who provide hosting, customer support, and app analytics (e.g., Firebase), subject to strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may disclose information to government authorities to protect our rights or the safety of our users.

4. Your Choices & Rights

You have control over the data you share:

  • Location Services: You can opt-out of location sharing through your device settings at any time; however, this will disable the automatic weather update feature for your alarms.
  • Profile Data: You can update or delete your Codename and Birthday directly within the app settings.
  • Data Access & Deletion: Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR/CCPA), you may have the right to request a copy of your data or ask for its permanent deletion. Please contact us at proateak@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain your Personal Data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Services. Location data used for weather queries is typically processed in real-time and not stored permanently on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to, or intended for, children under 13. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13. If you have reason to believe that a child under 13 has provided Personal Data to us through the Services, please email us. We will investigate any notification and, if appropriate, delete the Personal Data from our systems.

7. Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures (such as encryption) to protect your data. Please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use secure devices.
