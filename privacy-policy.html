Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

1. Scope and contact

Daylogin is provided by the developer of this app ("we" or "us"). This Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, provided and protected when you use the app, related policy websites or contact us, and how you can manage it. Contact: proateak@gmail.com.

No online account is required. Your codename, birthday, alarms, check-in records, custom broadcasts and imported audio are processed and stored on your device and are not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server through the app. Information handling for location and weather, policy-site requests and support emails is described separately below.

2. Information you provide and store on your device

Your local codename and birthday support personalized greetings, birthday reminders and day calculations. Alarm times, repeat days, enabled status, selected sounds and broadcasts support reminders. Check-in streaks and the most recent check-in time support login records. Custom broadcast titles, scripts, count-up and countdown dates, voice and music settings, today’s note, language, themes and other preferences are stored locally. You select imported audio through the system file picker; the app stores a converted copy, title and management information in its own storage without gaining access to all your files. No developer-operated online account is required. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information in scripts or support emails.

3. Location and weather

When you choose location detection and authorize it, the app requests your current location using iOS When In Use permission and uses Apple geocoding to identify the city and time zone. Location may include coordinates; accuracy depends on system authorization and may include precise location information. City, coordinates, time zone and weather cache are stored locally. Apple WeatherKit processes the relevant coordinates and request information for weather queries. The app does not continuously track movements in the background. Declining or disabling location does not prevent unrelated features. Disabling permission stops new system location access but does not automatically erase saved locations or weather caches; weather may still be requested for the saved location. Without a location fix, a default city may be used and its weather does not describe your actual location.

4. Alarms, speech and local diagnostics

iOS handles authorized alarm schedules and necessary reminder content. Notifications used on applicable versions are also subject to system notification settings. Broadcasts use iOS speech synthesis; the app does not send scripts to a developer-operated speech server. To reduce playback delay, the device may cache a synthesized opening clip, which may contain your codename or custom text. Local playback diagnostics record events, timing, language categories, success states and playback indices, not scripts, codenames or birthdays, and are not uploaded automatically. The log resets for a new broadcast and is limited to approximately 64 KB. The app does not request microphone, contacts, photo-library or health-data permissions.

5. Website access and language preference

On first launch, before the welcome screen appears, the app makes a network request to a policy website so iOS can handle applicable regional network permissions. Onboarding can continue whether or not the request succeeds. That request and subsequent webpage visits transmit the IP address, request path, time and necessary client request information to the website provider to deliver the request. They do not attach your local profile, custom broadcasts or imported audio.

When the device region is set to mainland China (CN), the app uses the norush.xin policy website; other device-region settings use the calmcache.github.io policy mirror. This selection follows device settings and does not identify the user’s actual location. The website stores your language preference locally in the browser. You can remove it by clearing website data.

6. Service providers and support information

The app does not integrate advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs or sell personal information. Location, weather, system alarms and speech use Apple services. Alibaba Cloud hosts the website, and GitHub Pages hosts the overseas policy mirror. These providers process the information needed to deliver their respective services.

When you contact proateak@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and attachments to respond, troubleshoot and handle related requests. This address uses Gmail. Provide only the information needed and obscure unrelated personal information in screenshots.

7. Purposes and provision of information

Local information supports the personalization, reminders, records and restoration of settings you choose. Location identifies a city and time zone and supports weather requests. Website requests deliver pages, enable communication and support necessary security maintenance. Support information is used for the purposes listed in Section 6. Where consent is the legal basis, we obtain the required consent. Processing that may legally occur without consent follows the applicable requirements.

Information we actually hold is provided only within the necessary scope permitted by law to meet legal obligations, comply with valid legal process, handle specific disputes or protect lawful rights. When entrusting processing to a provider, we specify the purpose, period, methods, information types, safeguards and respective obligations as required by law. Provision to another independent personal information processor is subject to required notice and separate consent where applicable.

For a merger, reorganization or business succession, we provide the recipient’s name and contact details as required and require it to continue the applicable protection obligations. If it changes the original purposes or methods, renewed consent is obtained where required. We do not publicly disclose your personal information except with valid authorization or as provided by law. These arrangements cover only information actually received or lawfully processed and do not automatically give us access to records on your device.

8. Retention and storage

Profiles, alarms, check-in records, scripts and audio are stored on your device. See Section 9 for deletion and backup management. The website uses Alibaba Cloud servers in Guangzhou, mainland China. The overseas policy mirror uses GitHub Pages, and support email uses Gmail; these latter two services involve processing outside mainland China.

We retain personal information actually received for the shortest period necessary for the purposes in this Policy. Information required by law or necessary to handle a specific dispute is retained as required. When those purposes and other retention grounds end, it is deleted or lawfully anonymized.

For cross-border provision of personal information, we provide the notice required by applicable law concerning overseas recipients, processing purposes, information types and rights procedures, obtain separate consent where legally required, and complete the required procedures.

9. Your choices, deletion and backups

You can edit your profile, manage alarms, delete custom broadcasts and imported ringtones, and manage permissions in iOS settings. Deleting a custom broadcast removes its entry and audio settings. Deleting an imported ringtone removes the app-managed audio file and entry, not the original source file. Removing one item does not clear your profile, check-in records, other preferences or all caches; a speech-opening cache may remain pending replacement or cleanup. There is no online-account closure or unified erase-all-data control. To remove local app data, use iOS Delete App, rather than Offload App, which retains documents and data. Manage system backups, restored copies and files copied outside the app separately; restoring a backup may restore earlier information. Revoking permission does not undo prior lawful processing or automatically clear existing data.

10. Your data rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, copy, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and obtain portability where applicable, and to complain to a competent authority. Local editing and permission controls are described above. For support or website-related information we actually hold, email proateak@gmail.com. We will use proportionate identity verification, respond within applicable statutory periods or explain lawful reasons for declining, and avoid unnecessary proof requests. We cannot remotely inspect, export or erase data stored only on your device, but can offer instructions. We will not impose unlawful discriminatory treatment for exercising rights.

11. Children and security

The Services are not directed to children under 13. Higher local age thresholds and special parental-consent requirements apply where required. Minors need a parent or guardian’s permission and guidance. In mainland China, personal information of children under fourteen is sensitive personal information. Where such information is processed, we obtain guardian consent and take the corresponding safeguards as required by law. If you believe a child has provided us with information requiring special legal protection, email us so we can investigate and respond lawfully. The app primarily relies on iOS app isolation and system permissions for local protection, and configured policy-site requests use HTTPS. Protect device unlock credentials and handle backups, audible broadcasts and email attachments carefully. No Internet or email transmission is completely secure. For security incidents involving information we process, we will take remedial, notification and reporting steps as required by law.

12. Changes to this Policy

We update this Policy and its date as features and information handling change, and provide appropriately prominent notice of material changes. Where additional consent is required, we obtain it before the relevant processing. A policy update is not blanket authorization for new processing. Contact proateak@gmail.com with questions.

本应用无需注册在线账号。代号、生日、闹钟、打卡记录、自定义播报与导入音频在设备上处理和保存，不会通过本应用自动上传至开发者服务器。定位与天气、政策网站请求以及客服邮件涉及的信息处理，分别按下文说明。

2. 您提供及保存在设备上的信息

本机档案包括代号和生日，用于个性化问候、生日提示和天数计算；闹钟时间、重复星期、启用状态、所选铃声和播报用于安排提醒；连续打卡次数及最近打卡时间用于登录记录。自定义播报标题、正文、正计时和倒计时日期、语音与配乐配置、今日备注，以及语言、主题等偏好保存在本机。您通过系统文件选择器主动选取音频，App 在自身存储空间保存转换后的副本、标题及管理信息，不因此取得对全部文件的访问权。无需注册开发者在线账号；请勿在文案或支持邮件中加入不必要的敏感信息。

3. 位置与天气

您选择定位并授权后，App 通过 iOS 使用期间位置权限请求当前位置，并调用 Apple 地理编码服务识别城市和时区。位置可能包含经纬度，实际精度取决于系统授权，可能包括精确位置信息。城市、坐标、时区和天气缓存保存于设备；查询天气时，Apple WeatherKit 处理相应坐标和请求信息。App 不持续在后台跟踪行踪。拒绝或关闭定位不妨碍不依赖位置的功能；关闭权限会停止新的系统定位，但不会自动删除已保存的位置或天气缓存，天气功能仍可能按保存位置查询。未取得定位时可能使用默认城市，其天气不代表您的实际位置。

4. 闹钟、语音与本地诊断

系统闹钟由 iOS 根据授权处理时间和必要提醒内容；适用版本中的通知也受系统通知设置控制。语音播报调用 iOS 语音合成，App 不向开发者的语音服务器提交文案。为减少播放等待，设备可能保存合成的开场语音缓存，音频中可能包含代号或自定义文案。播放诊断在本地缓存中记录事件、耗时、语言类别、成功状态和播放序号，不记录文案、代号或生日，不自动上传；诊断日志随新一轮播报重置，并受约 64 KB 大小限制。App 不请求麦克风、通讯录、相册或健康数据权限。

5. 网站访问与语言偏好

首次启动时，本应用会在欢迎页展示前向政策网站发起网络访问请求，供 iOS 处理适用地区的网络访问授权；联网是否成功不影响继续引导。请求及后续网页访问会向网站服务方传输完成通信所需的 IP 地址、访问路径、时间及必要的客户端请求信息，不附带您的本机档案、自定义播报或导入音频。

当设备地区设置为中国大陆（CN）时，本应用使用 norush.xin 政策网站；其他设备地区设置使用 calmcache.github.io 政策镜像。该选择依据设备设置，并非对用户实际所在地的识别。网站在浏览器本地保存语言偏好，您可通过清理网站数据删除。

6. 服务提供方与客服信息

本应用不接入广告、统计、归因或跟踪 SDK，不销售个人信息。定位、天气、系统闹钟及语音等功能使用 Apple 服务；官网使用阿里云托管，海外政策镜像使用 GitHub Pages。相关服务方处理提供相应服务所需的信息。

您通过 proateak@gmail.com 联系我们时，我们会收到您的邮箱、正文和附件，用于回复、排查问题及处理相关请求。该邮箱使用 Gmail 服务。请仅提供处理问题所需的资料，并遮挡截图中的无关个人信息。
