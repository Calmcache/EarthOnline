Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies only to Daylogin (app identifier com.earthonline.clock), maintained by NoRush. Other apps have their own policies. Contact: proateak@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Your codename, birthday, alarms, repeat days, selected sounds, check-in records, interface preferences, custom broadcast scripts and audio settings are stored on your device to provide these features. You choose imported audio through the system file picker; the app stores the required copy in its own storage. No online account is required.

3. Location and weather

With your permission, iOS location services provide a location for city lookup, weather and time zone settings. Accuracy depends on your system permissions. City, coordinates, time zone and a weather cache are stored locally. Apple WeatherKit and geocoding services process the necessary location parameters when you request weather or city information. You can manage location access in iOS settings; declining it does not prevent other features that do not rely on location.

4. Alarms and speech

System alarms use iOS alarm capabilities and require your permission. iOS processes the alarm schedule and necessary reminder content. Broadcasts use iOS speech synthesis; scripts and audio preferences are stored locally. The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photo library or health data.

5. Network and website access

On first launch, the app makes a normal request to its policy site before the welcome screen so iOS can handle applicable regional network permissions. iOS determines whether a prompt appears; onboarding does not require a successful network response to continue. When you visit the website or policy pages, your browser sends the IP address and browser request information necessary to deliver the page to the hosting server. The website stores a language preference on your device.

6. Services and information use

The current app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not upload profiles or custom content to a developer-operated server. Apple provides weather and location services under its relevant privacy terms. If you email us, your address, message and attachments you choose to provide are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Your choices and information management

Manage your profile, alarms, broadcasts and audio within the app, and location and alarm permissions in iOS settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data; system backups are managed through your device and backup settings. Support correspondence is retained for the time needed to answer and follow up on your request.

8. Updates

We update this page and its date when functionality or information handling changes, and provide appropriate notice for material changes. Contact proateak@gmail.com with questions.

隐私政策

更新日期: 2026-10-31

1. 适用范围与联系

本政策仅适用于早上号 Daylogin（App 标识 com.earthonline.clock），由莫慌网 NoRush 维护。不作为其他 App 的隐私说明。联系邮箱：proateak@gmail.com。

2. 保存在设备上的资料

你设置的代号、生日、闹钟、重复星期、铃声选择、登录记录、界面偏好、自定义播报文案及其音频配置保存在本机，用于提供对应功能。导入的音频由你通过系统文件选择器主动选择，App 将所需副本保存在自己的存储空间。Daylogin 无需注册在线账号。

3. 位置与天气

经你授权，App 通过 iOS 位置服务获取位置并反查城市，用于查询天气与设置城市时区。精度由系统授权决定。城市、经纬度、时区及天气缓存保存在设备本地；获取天气及城市信息时，相应位置参数由 Apple 的 WeatherKit 与地理编码服务处理。你可以在系统设置中管理位置权限；拒绝位置授权不会阻止使用其他不依赖定位的功能。

4. 闹钟与语音

系统闹钟通过 iOS 的闹钟能力执行，需你授予相应权限。闹钟时间及必要的提醒内容由 iOS 处理。播报使用 iOS 提供的语音合成功能，文案与音频偏好保存在本机。App 不要求麦克风、通讯录、相册或健康数据权限。

5. 联网与网站访问

首次启动会在欢迎页之前向政策网站发起普通联网请求，供系统处理适用地区的网络访问授权；是否显示弹窗由 iOS 决定，不以网络成功作为继续引导的条件。访问官网或政策页面时，浏览器会向托管服务器发送完成网页传输所需的 IP 地址和浏览器请求信息。网站在设备中记住语言偏好。

6. 第三方服务与数据使用

当前 App 未集成广告或第三方统计 SDK，不向开发者自建服务器上传个人档案或自定义内容。天气与定位服务由 Apple 提供，其信息处理规则以 Apple 的相关隐私说明为准。通过邮件联系我们时，你的邮箱、邮件正文及主动附带的资料用于回应问题。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。

7. 你的选择与数据管理

你可以在应用内修改资料并管理闹钟、播报和音频，在 iOS 设置中管理位置及闹钟权限。删除 App 会移除该 App 在本机的资料；系统备份的保留由你的设备和备份设置管理。联系我们处理的邮件资料，仅为回应及跟进问题保留所需时间。

8. 政策更新

功能或信息处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页及更新日期；重要变化会通过适当方式提示。对本政策或信息处理有疑问，请联系 proateak@gmail.com。
